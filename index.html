ReelPilot

A private scheduling tool for the social accounts of HKMK.

ReelPilot is a small self-hosted application that runs on the owner's own computer. It plans short videos on a calendar and publishes them to the owner's own social media accounts at the chosen time. It also reads back the reach and view counts of those posts so the owner can see how a video performed.

What it does

  • Schedules videos on a calendar, per platform.
  • Publishes them automatically at the planned time.
  • Shows views, reach and engagement for the published videos.

Who can use it

ReelPilot is not a public service and has no sign-up. It is used by its owner for the accounts that the owner controls: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok.
