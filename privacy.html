Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

ReelPilot ("the app") is a private, self-hosted tool that publishes videos to the social media accounts of its owner and reads back the statistics of those posts. This policy explains what data the app handles.

1. No data from other people

The app has no users other than its owner. It has no sign-up, no visitor accounts and no contact forms. It does not collect data from anyone visiting this website, and this website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

2. What data the app handles

  • Access tokens for the owner's own Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok accounts, granted through each platform's official login screen.
  • Basic account information of those accounts, such as the account name, account ID and profile picture, used to show which account a post belongs to.
  • The owner's own content: the video files, captions and scheduled times.
  • Statistics of the owner's own posts, such as views, reach, likes, comments and shares.

The app does not read other people's profiles, private messages, or the content of other accounts.

3. Where the data is stored

All of it stays on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain. Scheduling data and statistics are kept in a local database file. Nothing is sent to a server of the developer, because there is none, and nothing is sold, shared or handed to third parties.

4. Who the data is shared with

Data is exchanged only with the platforms themselves — TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube — in order to publish posts and read their statistics. Their own privacy policies apply to the data held on their side.

5. How long it is kept

Scheduling data and statistics are kept until the owner deletes them. Access tokens are kept until they expire or are revoked. Deleting the app's folder and revoking access in each platform's settings removes everything.

6. Withdrawing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the settings of the account concerned, for example under "Apps and services" in TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or Google. The app then loses all access to that account.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and is not used by anyone other than its owner.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always the one published on this page.
